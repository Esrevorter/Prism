## What is Prism, in one paragraph

Prism is digital money that keeps your finances **your business**. Every payment is private by default — nobody watching the network can see who paid whom, or how much. But unlike other private coins, Prism also lets you *choose* what to reveal: prove to your accountant that you reported all your income, or to a landlord that you have enough savings, **without exposing anything else**. And because self-custody has historically been scary and brittle, Prism's wallet is designed to be *forgiving* — lose a device? Recover with friends instead of a single doomsday password. Let an assistant handle boring recurring payments? Only within limits you set, with 24 hours to veto anything. It was designed around the life of an independent creative professional — irregular project income from around the world, lots of small payouts to collaborators, zero appetite for financial surveillance — but those features are useful for everyone.

## The five promises (and how they actually work)

<table>
<tr><td width="50%">

### 🔒 Private by default
Every transaction hides sender, receiver, and amount — using the same class of battle-tested cryptographic techniques that make Monero private (ring signatures, stealth addresses, hidden amount commitments), plus network-level relay protection so your IP isn't leaked when you pay.

### 🌈 Transparent by choice — *The Prism Protocol*
Need to prove something? Generate a **selective disclosure**: a mathematical proof of exactly one fact ("my balance exceeds X", "these funds didn't come from a sanctioned address") for exactly one recipient, with an expiry date. Your full ledger stays hidden. We're honest about the semantics: you can *limit and expire* disclosures, but nothing on earth can recall a proof someone already accepted.

</td><td width="50%">

### 🛟 Forgiving custody
Your spending authority is split into **key shares** spread across your own devices and up to four trusted friends (3-of-5). Lose your phone? Start recovery, get quorum approval, and funds stay locked for **72 hours** while every device you own screams warnings — anyone holding an old share can cancel it. No seed phrase required (classic seed phrases still work if you insist — clearly labeled as the irreversible option).

### 🤖 Helpful, never pushy AI
An on-device assistant turns *"Send $50 to Alex for the session, keep it private"* into an exact transaction, shows you a plain-language preview, flags scams, and waits for your biometric confirmation before anything happens. The AI **never signs anything itself**. Recurring jobs (like auto-converting spare change) run under explicit permission grants with a 24-hour veto window and a tamper-proof audit log.

### 💸 Pennies, not surprises
Sending costs a fixed base fee of **0.0001 PRSM** (~$0.001) — no auction, no math anxiety. In-app payment channels make tips and micropayments effectively free and instant.

</td></tr>
</table>

Think "engine on the test bench," not "car in the showroom." Do not expect to buy, sell, or mine PRSM yet; any coin claiming to be PRSM today is a scam.

## Try it yourself (no coding needed)

Releases include a **ready-to-run program** for Windows, macOS, and Linux — download from [Releases](https://github.com/prism-prsm/prism/releases), unzip, done. No installation, no Python required. Each archive contains its own `SHA256SUMS` file so you can verify the download wasn't tampered with.

**Windows:** download `prism-0.1.0-windows-x86_64.zip` → right-click → *Extract All…* → open the folder → type `cmd` in the address bar and press Enter. Then try:

```console
C:\> prism params
{ ...the money rules: 21M cap, 120 s blocks, 0.0001 PRSM base fee... }

C:\> prism genesis --network refraction
{ ...the first block of the planned test network, exactly reproducible... }

C:\> prism emit --heights 0,100,315360
[ ...how many PRSM exist at each height — the halving curve, visible... ]

C:\> prism mine --blocks 3
{ ...three demo blocks mined and validated end-to-end... }
```

Prefer pictures and step-by-step? Follow [Wiki → Getting Started as a User](wiki/Getting-Started-as-a-User.md).

## Privacy: what we can honestly promise

We'd rather tell you the truth than oversell. Full list in [Wiki → FAQ](wiki/FAQ.md); headline version:

✅ **We promise:** amounts, senders, and receivers hidden on-chain by default · no company ever holds your keys · disclosures are scoped, expiring, and logged locally so you always know what you revealed · biometric data never leaves your device · AI runs entirely on your hardware.

⚠️ **We don't promise:** perfect anonymity against a global adversary with unlimited resources (nobody can — even cash has limits) · recall of a proof you already handed over · recovery if you deliberately chose the legacy seed-only mode and lost the phrase.

## Frequently asked short answers

**Is Prism anonymous like Tor?** No — it's *private by default with user-controlled disclosure*, which is stronger for daily life and more honest about its limits.
**Who controls the network?** Miners and node operators. There is no company-held key, no admin switch, and no pre-mine.
**Can I recover a lost wallet?** Yes — unless you deliberately chose the irreversible seed-only mode.
**Can a government see my balance?** Not on-chain. They can compel *you* to disclose — which is exactly why the disclosure tooling exists: you control scope, recipient, and expiry.
**Is there a token sale?** No. Zero pre-mine, no sale, ever. Funding comes from transparent future emission only.
